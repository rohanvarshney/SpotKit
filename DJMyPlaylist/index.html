Harmonic Key Matching

Maps tracks onto the Camelot Wheel so adjacent songs share compatible keys (same key, relative major/minor, or ±1 fifth).

⏱️

BPM & Energy Flow

Minimizes abrupt tempo jumps between songs and supports smooth warm-up ramps, peak-hour arcs, or cooldown curves.

Optimal Set Ordering

Solves for the lowest-friction path across your playlist and saves the reordered sequence back to your Spotify account.
