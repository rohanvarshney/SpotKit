🚧 Work in Progress Interactive Branching Set Builder

Build your set one chosen track at a time.

MakeASet lets you load your Liked Songs and Playlists, narrow down the ideal opening track by genre, artist, title, or AI-assisted vibe description, and then click through curated next-track branches with instant revert ability.

🛠️
Currently Under Development (WIP) — Live Spotify OAuth library import and AI semantic track matching are being built. Below is an interactive concept preview demonstrating the starting-song filter, branching click-through choices, and step revert flow.
📥

Liked Songs & Playlists Input

Pull from your Spotify Liked Songs and any combination of your playlists to form the candidate pool for your set.

✨

Narrow the Starting Song

Filter by genre, artist, or title—or describe the mood in plain English with AI-assisted vibe matching to pick your opener.

🔀

Click-Through & Revert

Each chosen song reveals compatible follow-up options. Click to extend the set, or revert to any previous track to branch anew.
