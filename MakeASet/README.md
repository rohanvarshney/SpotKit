# MakeASet (WIP)

**MakeASet** is an upcoming interactive set-building tool in the `SpotKit` suite. You input your Spotify **Liked Songs** and **Playlists**, narrow down a starting song by genre, artist, title, or AI-assisted description, and then click through curated next-track options with full revert support to explore alternate branches.

> **Status**: Work in Progress (WIP preview and interactive concept prototype active at `MakeASet/index.html`).
